# OrderSnap 🍽️  
**Online Food Ordering System**

OrderSnap is a web-based application designed to make ordering food from favorite restaurants fast, simple, and convenient. 
Users can browse menus, place orders, and manage their dining preferences through an intuitive, user-friendly interface.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔍 What is OrderSnap?

OrderSnap connects customers to restaurants through a seamless digital platform. It allows users to:

- Register and log in securely
- Browse menus with images, prices, and descriptions
- Add food items to a cart and place orders
- View order history and manage current orders

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ⚙️ Core Features (CRUD Operations)

The heart of OrderSnap is its **Order Management System**, powered by CRUD operations:

- **Create** – Add new orders by selecting items and quantities  
- **Read** – View current cart and past orders with details  
- **Update** – Modify quantities of items in the cart  
- **Delete** – Remove unwanted items from the cart

These features ensure full control over the ordering experience.
